Unofficial fan tool. Not made or endorsed by the Tectonic author or Mojang. Terrain data from Tectonic 3.0.28 by Apollo (MIT). Source and issues on GitHub.

What matches the game: seeding, every noise value, and Tectonic's 2D terrain and climate functions, so a seed should line up with your world.

What is approximate: surface height leaves out the 3D roughness noise, caves and aquifer lakes, so heights can be off by a few blocks and some sea floors by more. Biomes are vanilla only. Wilder Wild, Terrestria and Biolith biomes are not shown.

Drag to pan, scroll or use the buttons to change scale. Click the map to pin a spot and get a teleport command.
